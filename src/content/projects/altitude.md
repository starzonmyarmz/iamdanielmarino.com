Altitude is a screen-printed stompbox with five floors: Cave, Valley, You, Ridge and Peak. Push up the low floors for organ-style bass lines, the high ones for shimmering pads, or all five for something huge.

Each octave is a granular pitch shifter. It costs almost no CPU and handles chords, at the price of a little chorus-like warble on long single notes and a few milliseconds of lag on the shifted floors. Tone and Detune help smooth it over, and it’s great for pads, bass layers and recorded parts.

- **Tone** darkens the octave floors without touching your dry signal.
- **Attack** fades the octaves back in after each pick attack, for swells.
- **Detune** nudges the floors slightly out of tune with each other.
- **Presets** step through saved stacks from the amber window.
