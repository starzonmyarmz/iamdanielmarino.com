Ghosted looks like a vintage bakelite radio with ghosts on the dial. Turn the Distance knob to tune from tight double-tracking out to slapback, and pick two ghosts (hard left and right) or four.

Each ghost drifts in time with smoothed random motion, which bends its pitch a little, like a second take that isn’t quite in time. Pick attacks nudge each ghost early or late for a moment, so they never land exactly together, and each one gets its own EQ so they sound like different takes rather than copies.

- **Haunt** sets the wet mix.
- **Drift** sets how far and how fast the ghosts wander.
- **Spook** sets how hard each pick attack pushes the ghosts early or late.

Knob moves glide, and fast Distance changes dip the pitch like tape instead of clicking.
