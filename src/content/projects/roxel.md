Roxel is a canvas-first editor: a single inspector on the left, a floating tool island, and everything else out of the way. Build on an open-world grid with no box to fit inside, as high as you like.

- **Tools** for brushing, erasing, recoloring, eyedropping, drawing shapes, selecting and moving, with 2D shapes that extrude into boxes and cylinders.
- **Palettes** including PICO-8, Sweetie 16, DawnBringer and Endesga, plus your own, with Adobe Swatch Exchange import and export.
- **Plays well with others.** Open MagicaVoxel, Qubicle and Goxel files, and export to MagicaVoxel, Goxel, OBJ, glTF (ready for Unity and Godot), PNG and SVG.
- **Light and dark themes**, an isometric camera you can orbit, pan and zoom, and an optional floor grid.

I built Roxel as a detour from making a game, and wrote about why in the posts below.
